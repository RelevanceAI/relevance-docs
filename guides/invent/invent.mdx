Every guide in this section describes a process: what goes in, which systems it touches, and what good output looks like. That is the same thing Invent asks you for, which makes a guide a useful starting script rather than something you have to translate.

## From a guide to a working Agent

1. Read the use case you care about and note where your version differs — your CRM, your stages, your definition of a good result
2. Open Invent and describe that process in your own terms, including the judgment calls you make today
3. Answer its questions about the parts you left out, then review what it drafts before anything goes live
4. Ask it to turn a real run into an Evals suite, so the Agent has a quality bar before it handles live work

<Tip>
  Describe the process you actually run, not the one in the guide. Invent builds against what you tell it, so your exceptions and edge cases are the useful part.
</Tip>

## Pick a process you know end to end

The guides are organized by role because the person who knows a process well is usually the one who runs it. Invent needs that knowledge from you — it can build and test the Agent team, but it cannot tell you how your team decides a lead is worth calling.
