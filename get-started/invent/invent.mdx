Invent is a forward deployed agent built into Relevance AI. You describe a business process in your own words, and Invent works through it with you before building and managing the Agents that run it.

That means you do not have to start in the builder. Agents, Tools, Workforces, and Knowledge are all things Invent can create, connect, and test on your behalf — you bring the knowledge of how the process actually works.

## What your first task looks like

1. You describe a process — the outcome, the systems it touches, and the calls that need judgment
2. Invent reads it back and asks about the parts you left out
3. It builds the Agents, Tools, and Triggers the process needs, landing changes in draft for you to review
4. It sets the checks the work has to pass, then keeps sampling live runs and proposing fixes after launch

## What to have ready

Invent works best on a process you know end to end and could explain out loud. Before you open it, be ready to say:

- The outcome you need, in the terms your team would use
- The systems the work touches, such as your CRM or your inbox
- The decisions that require judgment, and how you make them today
- How you would know the Agent did the job well

A rough description is enough to begin. Invent asks about the gaps rather than guessing at them.

## Before you start

<Note>
  Invent is available to organization admins by default. An organization admin can turn it on for everyone in an individual project from that project's settings.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Invent is expensive to run and consumes significant credits. Use it deliberately and keep an eye on your usage.
</Warning>
